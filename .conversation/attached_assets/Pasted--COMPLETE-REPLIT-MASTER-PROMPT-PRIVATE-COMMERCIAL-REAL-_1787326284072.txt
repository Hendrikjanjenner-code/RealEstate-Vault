# COMPLETE REPLIT MASTER PROMPT — PRIVATE COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE DEAL PLATFORM

Build a complete, production-ready, premium B2B SaaS marketplace for off-market commercial real estate.

The platform is intended for professional real estate brokers, investors, acquisition brokers, sellers, owners, developers, family offices, private equity firms, institutional investors, hotel operators and other professional buyers.

The core purpose is to facilitate confidential commercial real estate transactions from discovery through closing.

The platform must combine:

* Professional networking
* Commercial real estate listings
* Private deal discovery
* Investor mandates
* Broker discovery
* Broker ranking
* Deal matching
* Digital NDAs
* Virtual data rooms
* Secure document sharing
* Broker introduction protection
* Commission agreements
* Deal pipelines
* Messaging
* Notifications
* Verified transaction history
* Reputation scores
* AI-generated listing content
* AI-generated property videos
* Subscription management
* Admin tools
* Analytics

The platform must feel premium, institutional, secure, modern and trustworthy.

Do not build it like a consumer real estate website.

The target users transact assets worth millions or tens of millions of euros.

The experience should resemble the quality of:

* Institutional investment platforms
* Private equity software
* Investment banking portals
* Premium deal rooms
* High-end commercial real estate platforms

The product must be responsive and work well on desktop, tablet and mobile.

# CORE CONCEPT

The platform should revolve around the following transaction lifecycle:

**Deal Discovery → Interest → Broker Approval → NDA → Confidential Access → Data Room → Broker Protection → Commission Agreement → Due Diligence → Offer → Negotiation → Closing → Verified Transaction → Reputation**

This lifecycle should be reflected throughout the application.

# IMPORTANT FINAL INSTRUCTION

Do not treat this project as a simple real estate listing website.

The core value proposition is the infrastructure around confidential transactions.

The most important product pillars are:

**1. Private Deal Discovery**

**2. Verified Professional Network**

**3. NDA-Controlled Confidential Access**

**4. Secure Virtual Data Rooms**

**5. Broker Introduction Protection**

**6. Digital Commission Agreements**

**7. Investor Mandates**

**8. Broker Discovery & Ranking**

**9. Verified Transaction Reputation**

**10. AI-Powered Deal Marketing**

The product should make commercial real estate brokers more willing to share off-market opportunities because their introductions and commissions are protected.

It should make investors more willing to participate because the brokers, deals and transaction histories are verified.

The platform should become the trusted digital infrastructure between both sides.

Build this as a complete SaaS application with real workflows, proper permissions, a scalable database architecture and premium UX.
