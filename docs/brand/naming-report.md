## 3. Five finalists — mini brand concepts

### 1. CLOSA
- **Primary tagline:** From opportunity to close.
- **Secondary tagline:** The infrastructure behind private transactions.
- **Positioning:** The network where private opportunities become closed transactions.
- **Personality:** Confident, exact, quietly powerful.
- **Logo concept:** *The Close Mark* — a circle drawn as an open arc, sealed by a single straight terminal bar: the moment a deal closes, abstracted.
- **Icon concept:** The Close Mark alone; reads as a "C" at 16 px.
- **Typography:** Geometric-humanist sans; tabular numerals for financial data.
- **Colour:** Near-black ink, warm off-white, one jade accent.
- **Homepage headline:** "Private transactions, closed with certainty."
- **App icon:** Ink square, jade Close Mark centered.

### 2. VEYRA
- **Taglines:** The verified route. / Every deal, one trusted path.
- **Positioning:** The verified rail for private-market transactions.
- **Logo concept:** A path monogram — "V" formed by two converging strokes, one accent-coloured.
- **Typography:** Sharp grotesque; wide tracking in uppercase.
- **Colour:** Charcoal, bone white, electric cobalt accent.
- **Headline:** "The route every serious deal takes."

### 3. TORV
- **Taglines:** The private market square. / Where serious trade lives.
- **Positioning:** The modern market square for private assets.
- **Logo concept:** A square with one open corner — an entered marketplace.
- **Typography:** Nordic grotesque, generous whitespace.
- **Colour:** Graphite, chalk, moss accent.
- **Headline:** "A market square for private markets."

### 4. CREDON
- **Taglines:** Built on trust. / Where institutions transact.
- **Positioning:** Institutional-grade infrastructure for private transactions.
- **Logo concept:** Solid wordmark, no icon — pure typographic authority (Stripe-style).
- **Typography:** Neo-grotesque, heavy weight, tight tracking.
- **Colour:** Ink, ivory, deep signal green.
- **Headline:** "The standard for private transactions."

### 5. MONDRA
- **Taglines:** Private markets, worldwide. / One network. Every market.
- **Positioning:** The global network for private-market transactions.
- **Logo concept:** A circle bisected by a rising chord — a globe with a route across it.
- **Typography:** Rounded-corner grotesque, international flavour.
- **Colour:** Deep navy-charcoal, sand, coral accent.
- **Headline:** "Wherever private deals happen."

## 6. Recommendation — CLOSA

**CLOSA is the recommended master brand.**

1. **It owns the category's defining moment.** Every participant — investor, seller, advisor, financier — is on the platform for one reason: to get to the close. No asset class is named, so the brand scales to M&A, private debt, infrastructure and beyond.
2. **It passes every test.** Highest total score (69/80), natural in all six verbal tests, flawless brand architecture (Closa Deals, Closa Network, Closa Intelligence, Closa Verify, Closa Score all read as products of one company), and the only finalist with genuine verb potential.
3. **International by construction.** Five letters, two syllables, pronounced identically in English and Dutch, Latin-rooted so Romance-language markets read it instantly, and strong in uppercase: **CLOSA**.
4. **It carries the personality.** Confident and final without being aggressive; institutional without being dusty; modern without being crypto.

**Identity summary (implemented in the shared brand package):**
- **Name:** Closa — **Wordmark:** CLOSA
- **Primary tagline:** From opportunity to close.
- **Secondary tagline:** The infrastructure behind private transactions.
- **Logo:** The Close Mark — an open arc sealed by a terminal bar.
- **Palette:** Ink `#0C0D0E`, Charcoal `#17191C`, Warm off-white `#F6F4EF`, accent Jade `#14A87D`. Colour used sparingly; no gradients, no gold.
- **Typography:** Sora (display/headings), Inter (UI, body, tabular financial data).
- **First vertical:** Closa Real Estate — the master brand stays asset-agnostic.
