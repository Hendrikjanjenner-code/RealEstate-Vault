## 6. Recommendation — SOVRA (against benchmark CLOSA)

**SOVRA is the strongest name of round 2 (69/80), matching Closa's round-1 score.**

1. **It owns the category's defining trait.** Private markets are *sovereign* markets — closed, self-governing, invitation-based. SOVRA names that world without naming any asset class, so it scales to M&A, private debt and infrastructure untouched.
2. **It passes every test.** Flawless brand architecture (Sovra Deals, Sovra Network, Sovra Intelligence, Sovra Verify all read as one company), natural in all six verbal tests, five letters, two syllables, identical pronunciation in Dutch and English, and commanding in uppercase: **SOVRA**.
3. **It moves the brand upmarket.** Where Closa is about the *moment* (the close), Sovra is about the *domain* (the sovereign market). Closa is warmer and more verb-able ("we closa'd it"); Sovra is more institutional and more exclusive.

**Honest head-to-head:**

| | CLOSA (round 1) | SOVRA (round 2) |
|---|---|---|
| Total score | 69/80 | 69/80 |
| Core idea | The moment of closing | The sovereign private market |
| Tone | Confident, kinetic | Composed, authoritative |
| Verb potential | Yes ("closa'd it") | Limited |
| Institutional gravitas | High | Higher |
| Implementation cost | Zero (already live) | One rebrand pass |

The round-2 process produced one genuine peer to Closa, not a clear successor. **If the brand should feel more institutional and exclusive: choose SOVRA. If speed, verb-ability and the momentum of the existing identity matter more: keep CLOSA.** The final call is the founder's.
