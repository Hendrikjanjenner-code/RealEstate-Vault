# Replit Master Prompt — Rebrand Real Estate Vault Into a Scalable Global Brand

I want you to completely rebrand the existing **Real Estate Vault** application.

IMPORTANT:

Do **not** rebuild the application.

Do **not** remove or change existing functionality, workflows, database architecture, permissions, NDA system, data rooms, broker network, Deal Health, professional marketplace or transaction functionality.

This task is specifically about creating and implementing a **new global brand identity** for the platform.

---

# 1. THE PROBLEM

The current name:

**Real Estate Vault**

is too descriptive and too narrowly focused on real estate.

The platform is becoming much larger than a real estate listing or data-room product.

It is evolving into infrastructure for high-value private transactions.

The platform connects:

* Deals
* Investors
* Brokers
* Sellers
* Buyers
* Capital
* Financing
* Lawyers
* Tax advisors
* Valuers
* Due diligence professionals
* Documents
* NDAs
* Data rooms
* Offers
* Commission agreements
* Deal teams
* Transaction workflows
* AI analysis
* Deal intelligence
* Verified transaction history
* Professional reputation

Therefore, the brand should NOT contain generic terms such as:

Real Estate

Property

Prop

Estate

Vault

Broker

Capital

Dealroom

Realty

The new name should be broad enough to eventually support other private-market transactions as well.

# 3. NAMING REQUIREMENTS

Generate potential brand names that preferably:

* Contain 4–7 letters
* Have 1–2 syllables
* Are easy to pronounce in English

* Work as an app icon

* Can become a verb in conversation
* Feel premium
* Feel modern

* Feel technological
* Feel institutional
* Are not explicitly tied to real estate

Prefer invented or semi-invented words.

Avoid obvious combinations of existing industry terms.
