# REPLIT PROMPT — EXPAND REAL ESTATE VAULT WITH AI DEAL ADVISORY & PROFESSIONAL DEAL TEAMS

Expand the existing **Real Estate Vault** application with a comprehensive **AI Deal Advisory, Deal Health and Professional Deal Team ecosystem**.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT rebuild the application from scratch.

Do NOT replace the existing Real Estate Vault functionality.

Extend the existing architecture, database, user system, deal pages, NDA workflow, data rooms, broker marketplace, transaction pipeline and permissions.

The purpose of this expansion is to turn Real Estate Vault from a private commercial real estate marketplace into a complete transaction infrastructure platform.

The expanded transaction journey should become:

**Discover → Connect → NDA → Data Room → Analyse → Assemble Deal Team → Finance → Due Diligence → Offer → Negotiate → Close → Verify → Reputation**

The platform must combine AI automation with qualified human professionals.

AI should:

* Read
* Extract
* Organise
* Calculate
* Compare
* Summarise
* Detect missing information
* Flag potential risks
* Prepare questions
* Prepare reports
* Prepare professional review

AI must NOT be presented as a replacement for lawyers, tax advisors, valuers, financing specialists, notaries or other regulated professionals.

Where professional judgement is required, the system should clearly allow the user to request professional review.

# 61. CORE PLATFORM PHILOSOPHY

Real Estate Vault should no longer stop after connecting a buyer and seller.

It should support the entire commercial real estate transaction.

The platform journey becomes:

**DISCOVER**

Find off-market deals.

↓

**ACCESS**

Verification + NDA + secure data room.

↓

**ANALYSE**

AI analyses available financial, legal, operational and transaction information.

↓

**IDENTIFY**

Missing documents, attention points and areas requiring professional review are identified.

↓

**ASSEMBLE**

Build the professional Deal Team.

↓

**VERIFY**

Qualified professionals review relevant issues.

↓

**FINANCE**

Prepare the transaction for financing.

↓

**DUE DILIGENCE**

Manage financial, legal, tax, technical and commercial DD.

↓

**TRANSACT**

Offers, negotiations, commission protection and agreements.

↓

**CLOSE**

Complete and verify the transaction.

↓

**BUILD REPUTATION**

Brokers and professionals build verified track records.
